The app uses the microphone continuously, including in the background (UIBackgroundModes: audio), to detect drone propeller sound and alert the user with a local notification. Audio is processed on the device in real time; it is never recorded, stored or transmitted. No account is needed.

How to test:
1. Launch the app and pick a surroundings option (Indoors, City or Open area), then tap Continue.
2. Tap "Start listening" and allow microphone and notification access. Keep the phone still for 3 seconds while it learns the background sound.
3. Play a recording of a quadcopter drone flying (for example a drone flyby video) near the device. The status changes to "Drone sound" and an entry appears in the Log tab.
4. Send the app to the background and play the recording again: a time-sensitive notification with an SOS tone appears.
Tap "Stop listening" to stop using the microphone.

# Acoustic Drone Detector – Privacy Policy

Acoustic Drone Detector listens through your phone's microphone to detect drone propeller sound.

- Audio is never recorded, stored or sent off your device. All processing, including the on-device
  YAMNet audio model, happens on the phone.
- The app collects no personal data; there are no accounts, ads or analytics.
- The detection log, settings and ignored-sounds list are stored only on your phone and are removed
  when you delete the app.
- The microphone is used only after you tap "Start listening". While listening, Android shows a persistent
  notification and iOS shows the microphone indicator.
- Notification permission is used only to deliver drone alerts.

Contact: https://github.com/zeitries-ops/acoustic-drone-detector-ios/issues
